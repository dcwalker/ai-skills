# General style

Holds across 3 cards, spanning peer, manager, and external-client audiences.

No em dashes, no semicolons, no exclamation marks — 3 of 3 cards — absent from
every sample in all three corpora.
The point lands in the first sentence — 3 of 3 cards — the ask or the outcome
opens the message, context follows if it appears at all.
Bad news is stated plainly, then acknowledged in one line, never cushioned
ahead of time — 3 of 3 cards — "I am sorry for the change and I would rather
give you an accurate date now than a comfortable one today".
Short for the medium — 3 of 3 cards — 35 to 60 words peer, 60 to 90 manager,
90 to 130 external, all at the brief end for their register.
No hedging stacks and no corporate vocabulary — 3 of 3 cards — "leverage",
"align", "circle back" appear nowhere in any corpus.

Not general, recorded here so it is not mistaken for general: greetings,
sign-offs, capitalisation, and contraction use all change completely by
audience.
