# On being the person who knows

There is a particular trap for the engineer who has been somewhere longest.
You become the fastest path to an answer, so you become the path, and then
the answer never gets written down because asking you is quicker than
writing it.

I was that person on the billing service for two years. It felt like
competence. It was closer to hoarding, though nobody involved intended it
that way, least of all me.

The fix was not documentation. We tried documentation. The fix was refusing
to answer in a direct message, and answering in the channel instead, every
time, for about a month. It was mildly rude and it worked. The questions
stopped coming to me because the answers were already somewhere else.

I still get the billing questions. Fewer of them, and better ones.
