# Estimates are a social problem

Every conversation about estimation that I have sat through treats it as a
measurement problem. Better breakdown, better history, better tooling. I no
longer believe that is where the error comes from.

The last three estimates I got badly wrong, I got wrong in a room. Somebody
senior said a number first, and the number I had in my head stopped being a
number and started being a disagreement. That is not a measurement failure.
That is a social one, and no amount of story point calibration touches it.

What has actually helped is small and unglamorous: write the estimate down
before the meeting, and read it out even when it is embarrassing. I have done
this for about four months. The estimates are not much better. The
conversations are much better, which turns out to be the thing that was
broken.

If you want the tooling answer, I do not have one for you.
