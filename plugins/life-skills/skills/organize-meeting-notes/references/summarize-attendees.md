# Attendees Without a Full List

Read this in Step 3 when more than 20 people attended, or when the names of
everyone who attended cannot be established (the invite hides its guest
list, and the transcript, chat, and screenshots show only some of the
people who joined). A partial list of names reads as complete, so it
misleads.

## What Replaces the Roster

Skip the question about who did not attend, apply no strikethrough, and
list no names. The Attendees section is one line naming who was invited and
how many attended:

```markdown
###### Attendees
Tech Department invited, 56 attendees
```

- **Who was invited**: the group or distribution list the invite was sent
  to, such as `engineering-all@example.com`. When the invite names several,
  list each.
- **How many attended**: the number of people who joined, as the meeting
  platform or a screenshot reports it. When the report gives a peak
  concurrent count rather than a total, say so (`312 attendees at peak`).
  When no count is available, ask the user for it rather than estimating.

Count people who attended, not people invited: 56 invited with 15 attending
is 15 attendees, and the Step 3 roster applies when all 15 are known by name.
